Why these never expire

Many QR sites give you a code that points at their server, which then forwards people to your link. Stop paying and the code stops working.

These codes contain your link itself. There's nothing in between, so they'll keep working for as long as your link does — even if this site disappears. Codes are made in your browser; the link you paste is never sent anywhere.
